Final product identity

ChordKeep combines chord + keep: a home for your personal chord-sheet collection. The architectural meaning of a keep also connects subtly with the royal emblem.

Royal Folio

Approved product emblem

A three-point crown above an open folio with a pair of musical notes. The crown expresses sovereignty and purpose; the notes express music; the pages express a carefully kept collection of chord sheets. It is distinct from RUACH's Living Breath parent mark.

Used in the launcher, splash, homepage/setlist top bar and account dialog. The top-bar icon is hidden when viewing a song or chord sheet.
